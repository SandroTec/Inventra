# Vision

## Project Name

Inventra

---

## Vision Statement

Inventra is a modern inventory and asset management platform designed for small and medium-sized businesses.

Our goal is to replace scattered spreadsheets and manual documentation with an intuitive, reliable and scalable web application.

Inventra should help organizations keep track of their physical assets, improve transparency and reduce administrative effort.

---

## Mission

Build software that is:

- Easy to understand
- Fast to use
- Pleasant to work with
- Scalable
- Maintainable

The focus is not on offering hundreds of features, but on solving real business problems with a clean user experience.

---

## Core Values

- Simplicity over complexity
- User first
- Clean code
- Continuous improvement
- Performance
- Accessibility
- Maintainability

---

## Long-Term Vision

Inventra should evolve into a modular business platform.

Possible future modules include:

- Asset Management
- Maintenance Management
- Supplier Management
- Document Management
- Fleet Management
- Reporting & Analytics
- Notifications
- Mobile Companion App

---

## Success Criteria

A successful version of Inventra should:

- be enjoyable to use
- simplify daily workflows
- scale with business growth
- demonstrate modern software engineering practices
